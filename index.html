Detailed Component Feedback & Steps to Improve

Planned Response (PR) Analysis Content & Organization
Strengths: Clear expression of initial stance in response to the prompt.
Key Area for Growth: Deepen elaboration by connecting video observations directly to personal reasons.
Recommended Step to Improve: Ensure you explicitly mention at least 2 distinct details from the video clip (e.g., skater interactions, safety equipment) to anchor your argument.
Delivery & Rhythm Analysis Fluency & Intonation
Strengths: Consistent pacing and intelligible pronunciation throughout speech.
Key Area for Growth: Vary vocal intonation to highlight key persuasive arguments.
Recommended Step to Improve: Pause briefly before major transition words (e.g., 'Furthermore', 'Consequently') and emphasize key adjectives.
Question 1 (Personal Application) Spoken Interaction
Strengths: Directly answers prompt using relatable personal context.
Key Area for Growth: Expand on practical details and specific benefits in your proposed solution.
Recommended Step to Improve: Use the 'PREP' method (Point, Reason, Example, Point) to add concrete real-life examples to your response.
Question 2 (Societal Perspective) Critical Thinking
Strengths: Acknowledges broader community or societal dimensions of the topic.
Key Area for Growth: Consider multiple contrasting viewpoints (e.g., individual vs. community/government level).
Recommended Step to Improve: Incorporate discourse markers like 'On the one hand... On the other hand...' to show balanced evaluation.
